# Kuwot privacy policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Kuwot is a daily quote app made by fiatcode. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: Kuwot has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it does not collect personal information. The only data that leaves your device is an error report when the app crashes or hits an error.

## Data stored on your device

Kuwot saves a few things on your device so it can pick up where you left off:

- the last page and quote you viewed
- your settings, such as the theme and which quote groups you chose

This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

## Error reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends an error report to a crash-reporting server that we run ourselves. A report contains:

- the error message and the place in the code where it happened
- the app version
- technical details about the device, such as its model, Android version, language, screen size and free memory
- a random ID created when the app is installed, which is not linked to who you are
- the recent app events that led up to the error

A report does not contain your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information. We use error reports only to find and fix bugs. We do not sell or share them, and we keep them only as long as we need them to fix the problem.

## Google Play

Kuwot uses Google Play to check for app updates. Google Play handles any payment made through it. When you use Google Play, Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## Links to other websites

Some buttons in the app open a website in your browser, for example the source code on GitHub or a tip page on Buy Me a Coffee. Kuwot does not send any data to these sites. Their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes an image of the page on your device and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Kuwot never sends it anywhere by itself.

## Children

Kuwot is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
